I am a Ph.D. student in **Mathematics**, with a concentration in Actuarial and Risk Management Sciences, at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I am advised by [Professor Zhiyu Quan](https://www.zhiyuquan.net/). My research develops data-driven methods for risk assessment, with a particular interest in **climate risk**, **catastrophe risk**, and **insurance loss analytics**.

Before beginning my doctoral studies, I earned an M.S. in **Predictive Analytics and Risk Management** at Illinois and a B.S. in **Statistics** from Dalian Maritime University. I currently serve as a graduate supervisor for the Climate Data Innovation and Consolidation projects at the [Illinois Risk Lab](https://asrm.illinois.edu/illinois-risk-lab), in collaboration with the Society of Actuaries.

My work aims to turn complex, incomplete data into transparent evidence for underwriting, mitigation, and risk management. I welcome conversations about research collaboration at the intersection of actuarial science, insurance, and applied machine learning.
